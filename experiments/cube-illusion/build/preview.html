C64 cube font and illusion borders

The supplied cube, normalized to one foreground. Border variants retain the core and add independent one-pixel flanks. Both cubes share one timer; the right cube exchanges the flank roles.

Codes: 00 background ($D021); 01 core ($D022); 10 leading flank ($D023); 11 trailing flank (Color RAM). Each multicolor pixel is two screen pixels wide.

The default cycle advances through six stages and wraps to the beginning. A single counter drives $D022, $D023, and the trailing border's Color RAM at different phases. Color RAM switches between 8 and 9, keeping multicolor enabled.

The old bouncing ramp is retained for comparison. It reverses its brightness progression halfway through. These timings are not a measured reconstruction of the original video. Use the frozen view to inspect the fixed geometry.
