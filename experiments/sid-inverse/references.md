## Preparation and checks

`assets/prepare.py` reproduces the prepared targets. `assets/manifest.json`
records the exact source URLs, source and output SHA-256 hashes, source formats,
processing parameters, measured pitches, and fitting/holdout roles.

The process averages source channels to mono, resamples with a polyphase
low-pass filter to 22050 Hz, removes DC, and retains two milliseconds before
the first 2%-of-peak threshold crossing. Each output keeps at most the first
0.8 seconds from that point, with zero padding if necessary. An eight
millisecond cosine-squared tail fade avoids a discontinuity. Peak amplitude
is normalized to 0.95, and output uses 16-bit PCM. No pitch shifting occurs.

All four outputs were decoded and checked for mono audio, 22050 Hz rate,
17640 frames, finite samples, and matching manifest hashes. The four originals
plus four prepared clips occupy 708199 bytes of audio data. Download archives
are cached in an ignored `.downloads/` directory.

The guitar recordings are slightly sharp and their pitch changes during the
attack. Four 150 ms autocorrelation windows, starting at 50, 150, 250, and
350 ms in the source, give median fundamentals of approximately 221.4764 Hz
for A3 and 262.9694 Hz for C4. Individual estimates and normalized
autocorrelation peaks are retained in the manifest. Use these measured roots
when comparing rendered waveforms; nominal equal-tempered notes are also
recorded. The bass drum and snare have no assigned tonal root.

# Existing inverse-SID implementation: sidmatch

Primary project: <https://github.com/JC-000/c64-sid-instruments>.
Inspected source snapshot: `2af3d5cb422bb523ed0ea624539391a0c44646fe`.
This audit was a source inspection, not execution of that project.

The useful precedent is its search loop: render a candidate SID patch, compare
it with a reference recording, and optimize the parameters. It uses pyresidfp
for inner-loop rendering, discrete waveform/filter screening, and CMA-ES or
TPE refinement. Its patch model includes ADSR and time-varying waveform,
pulse-width, and filter controls. Its feature loss compares spectral content,
envelopes, pitch, and attack transients; multi-note fitting tests whether the
instrument generalizes across pitches.

- Pipeline documentation:
  <https://github.com/JC-000/c64-sid-instruments/blob/2af3d5cb422bb523ed0ea624539391a0c44646fe/tools/sidmatch/README.md>.
- Rendering and parameter model:
  <https://github.com/JC-000/c64-sid-instruments/blob/2af3d5cb422bb523ed0ea624539391a0c44646fe/tools/sidmatch/render.py>.
- Loss implementation:
  <https://github.com/JC-000/c64-sid-instruments/blob/2af3d5cb422bb523ed0ea624539391a0c44646fe/tools/sidmatch/fitness.py>.
- Multi-note evaluation:
  <https://github.com/JC-000/c64-sid-instruments/blob/2af3d5cb422bb523ed0ea624539391a0c44646fe/tools/sidmatch/multi_note.py>.

## Reuse decision and limits

Reuse the general optimization idea and compare results with these public
examples. Do not treat the project as a verified drop-in export engine.
No third-party optimizer code was copied during this asset/references task.

- The SID-Wizard encoder explicitly raises `NotImplementedError`, despite the
  main README listing SID-Wizard output:
  <https://github.com/JC-000/c64-sid-instruments/blob/2af3d5cb422bb523ed0ea624539391a0c44646fe/tools/sidmatch/encoders/sidwizard.py>.
- The ACME encoder emits at most 16 pulse-sweep rows and 16 filter-sweep rows.
  Exported playback must therefore be compared with the optimizer's rendered
  parameter trajectory before assuming they sound the same:
  <https://github.com/JC-000/c64-sid-instruments/blob/2af3d5cb422bb523ed0ea624539391a0c44646fe/tools/sidmatch/encoders/raw_asm.py>.
- GoatTracker export code exists, but its audible round-trip fidelity was not
  verified here:
  <https://github.com/JC-000/c64-sid-instruments/blob/2af3d5cb422bb523ed0ea624539391a0c44646fe/tools/sidmatch/encoders/goattracker.py>.
- Its README explicitly licenses the instruments under CC-BY 4.0. A separate
  conventional software license for optimizer-code reuse was not established
  in this inspection.
- A good fit to one isolated note does not establish quality across notes,
  durations, chip models, or a complete song. SID filter settings are shared
  across voices, so individually fitted patches can conflict in a mix.
- An emulator fit is evidence about that emulator and configuration. Hardware
  validation and an exported C64 player remain separate checks.
